Principal's Message

We are delighted to welcome you to Dharmagiri Group of Institutions, home to Edmount Dharmagiri for boys.

Here at Edmount, we are inspiring young men to dream big and achieve great things. But education for us is creating brilliant, responsible, and kind-hearted individuals who are ready to take on the world with confidence and compassion.

We are proud to offer a learning environment that's both challenging and nurturing, where every boy is encouraged to discover his strengths and reach his full potential. Together, we'll guide your son on a journey that's not only about academic success but also about building character and lifelong skills.

We can't wait to see what your son will accomplish as part of our Edmount family.

Warm regards,

Principal

Our Vision
